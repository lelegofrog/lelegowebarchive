Giggliest Monster on Sesame Street
Zoe is a furry, three-year-old girl monster who is so excited about everything that she can't get her words out fast enough. She and Elmo both love to laugh and sing, which is probably why they're best friends.

Birthday: September 30

Favorite Songs: "Let's Go Crazy"; "Put on a Happy Face"; "Jump"

Best Friend: Elmo

Quote: "Don't joke me!"

Likes: Mimi, her dolly; red monsters who giggle

Dislikes: Sitting still and the training wheels on her bicycle
 
 
 

Horoscope: Like many Libras, Zoe is simultaneously dainty and strong, practical and impulsive, and shares her sign with Barbara Walters ("If you were a Muppet, which one would you be?") and Nietzsche (the cute, furry, philosopher?).

Scientific Name: Teenius exciticus

Hobbies: Collecting necklaces, bracelets, and barrettes

Voice and Puppetry: Fran Brill
