Trashiest Talker on Sesame Street
No one enjoys a rainy day more than Oscar, who's known for being disagreeable. In spite of his bad attitude, Oscar teaches that those with different points of view also have a right to be heard. By collecting everyone else's junk, Oscar winds up helping out in his own neighborhood: the more junk he collects, the cleaner the streets get.

Birthday: June 1

Favorite Songs: "I Love Trash"; "All You Need is Trash"; "I Wanna Hold Your Trash"

Best Friends: Slimey the Worm and Maria (though he wouldn't admit it to her)

Quote: "Scram!"

Likes: Rainy days; arguing; standing in line at the movies (not seeing movies, just standing in line); anchovy milkshakes

Dislikes: "You name it, I hate it. Now go away."
 
 
 

Horoscope: Oscar's dual Gemini personality keeps him on his toes—he is either happy that he's miserable or miserable that he's happy. He shares his sign with Alanis Morissette (who has a similar problem), Bob Dylan (who looks a little like Oscar, if you squint), and John Fitzgerald Kennedy ("Ask not what Oscar can do for you, but what you can do for Oscar.")

Scientific Name: Trashius mouthicus

Philosophy: Leave me alone, and I'll leave you alone.

Hobbies: Collecting trash ("But only if it's really rotten.")

Voice and Puppetry: Caroll Spinney
